The DMT Manifesto

How Linamandla Thutha, Yanga Deliwe, and Zukolwethu Mdolomba govern software engineering.

01

Built to Last, Not Just to Ship

Working software isn't the finish line—software that scales without requiring a rebuild in a year is. We treat every line of code as if our own business depended on it.

02

Partners, Not Vendors

We inspect how your business operates before writing code. A boutique clinic and a logistics fleet run differently; we will never force template solutions onto operational problems.

03

Honest Scope, Honest Price

Fixed-scope guarantees before work begins. No hidden line-items, no scope creep disguised as upgrades, and zero invoice surprises.

04

Ubuntu Core Philosophy

We succeed only when our clients succeed. Long after going live, we remain accessible because long-term local business growth is our true measure of success.
